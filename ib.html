Biology IA

Urea Concentration & Chlorophyll Content in Water Sprite

Investigated how different concentrations of urea (2.00–10.00 ppm) affect the chlorophyll-a and chlorophyll-b content in Ceratopteris thalictroides. Used spectrophotometric measurements after 9 days of exposure to quantify light absorbance and infer photosynthetic impact under nitrate pollution conditions.

Surface Area of a Traditional Chinese Porcelain Vase
