About Me

I grew up in China, completed high school in Singapore, and now am pursuing Mathematics & Biostatistics major, neuroscience minor at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of computational neuroscience, biostatistics, differential equations (ODE/PDE), and multi-agent systems in quantitative finance. I'm passionate about combining AI and biological systems to model complexity, explore dynamics, and uncover hidden structures in real-world data.
