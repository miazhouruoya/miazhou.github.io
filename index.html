✦ datathon & hackathon

Best Statistical Analysis Winner – Duke ASA DataFest 2026
Mar 2026 · 5-person team · 48-hour competition

What I did

  • Led a 5-person team to analyze 10k+ diabetic patient records in a 48-hour competition. Directed end-to-end analytical pipeline on a diabetes-anchored cohort, engineering longitudinal EHR features including inter-visit gap sequences, cv_gap irregularity metric, and ED utilisation flags with SDOH risk stratification across 8 social determinant domains.
  • Surfaced structural health inequality via silhouette-optimised K-Means (k=3) and logistic regression — High-SDOH patients show 2.5× higher ED rates and concentrate 5× more in the ED-Dependent archetype (p<0.001). Counterfactual simulation quantified a 16.6pp risk reduction if structural barriers were removed.
  • Translated findings into a geospatial mobile clinic intervention: optimised routing circuit covering 32,686 patients across 15 stops. Integrated team's Markov Chain comorbidity network and MLP risk-scoring dashboard into a unified live-pitch presentation.
